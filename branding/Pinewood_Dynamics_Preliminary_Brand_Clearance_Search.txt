No exact, clearly relevant marketplace or indexed trademark use of PINEWOOD DYNAMICS was surfaced in the searches performed. That is favorable compared with GRAVITY RACER.

Because the proposed product is software about wooden hobby race cars, PINEWOOD DYNAMICS sits semantically between these two fields. The absence of an exact-match result does not eliminate likelihood-of-confusion, dilution, sponsorship, descriptiveness, or common-law risk.

MATERIAL ADJACENT RIGHTS

A. PINEWOOD DERBY
Indexed trademark records report PINEWOOD DERBY as a registered and renewed mark owned by Boy Scouts of America/Scouting America, Registration No. 2947271, covering hobby craft wooden toy automobile kits in Class 028 and entertainment services involving hobby craft automobile racing competitions in Class 041.

This is highly relevant because the proposed product designs and simulates the same general type of physical hobby vehicle and may be offered as educational/game software. Even though the candidate does not include DERBY, PINEWOOD is the dominant shared first word and the product may be marketed in the same hobby ecosystem.

B. PINEWOOD AUTOMOTIVE SOFTWARE / PINEWOOD.AI
Pinewood Technologies Group PLC publicly describes Pinewood.AI as a cloud-based automotive intelligence/software platform for automotive retailers and OEMs. Indexed U.S. and EU trademark records report PINEWOOD AUTOMOTIVE INTELLIGENCE in software, technology, vehicle, automotive-service and related classes. A Microsoft Store listing also exists for Pinewood DMS Companion from Pinewood Technologies PLC.

This is relevant because the proposed brand would be used for design/simulation software connected to cars, even though miniature gravity-racing cars differ from dealership-management and OEM software.

C. OTHER PINEWOOD MARKS
Indexed results also show PINEWOOD registrations in film/television studio and consulting fields. These are less closely related but reinforce that PINEWOOD is already used as a commercial source identifier by multiple parties.

D. DYNAMICS FIELD
The word DYNAMICS is widely used in engineering/software branding and is conceptually descriptive of physics and motion. Indexed results also show a SIMULATION DYNAMICS mark/use for industrial modeling and simulation consulting. This does not create an exact phrase conflict but reduces the distinctiveness contributed by DYNAMICS.

DESCRIPTIVENESS / REGISTRABILITY CONCERN
For software that simulates the physics/dynamics of pinewood-style cars, PINEWOOD DYNAMICS describes or strongly suggests the subject matter and function. This may weaken the mark and increase the chance that protection would be narrow. The phrase may be more protectable as a distinctive composite logo, but logo protection would not necessarily provide broad exclusivity in the words.
