GRAVITY RACER - EXTERNAL KNOWNS
AWANA / CCCS GRAND PRIX RULE BASELINE
Source accessed: https://cc-awana.weebly.com/rules--tips.html
Source title: Rules & Tips - AWANA at CCCS

PURPOSE
This file records externally sourced facts that can be treated as known inputs to the current Gravity Racer CCCS/AWANA ruleset. It intentionally separates published facts from design assumptions.

ELIGIBILITY / EVENT RULES
- Race is for Sparks and T&T clubbers.
- Entrant must be present for the car to compete in race and design contests.
- Cars must be constructed from the Official Awana Grand Prix Car Kit.
- Previously raced cars are not eligible; participating cars must be built specifically for this race.
- The kit axles, wheels, and wood supplies must be used.
- Additional features such as windshields, weights, and decals may be added, and the starting wood block may be modified.
- The Awana name must remain visible on the wheel sidewall.
- Wheel modifications may not reduce wheel width, diameter, or mass/weight.
- The front must not come to a point or slope upward in a way that prevents proper retention behind the starting peg.
- Chassis/underside clearance must be at least 3/8 inch to avoid the guide rail.
- No alterations are allowed after check-in, including final preparation and lubrication.
- If an entrant cannot attend check-in, another person may submit the car.
- Entrants must participate in designing and building their cars; the car should not be completely designed and built by an adult.
- Every car must pass technical inspection, including weigh-in and size check.

CAR SPECIFICATIONS
- Maximum length: 7.000 in
- Maximum width: 2.750 in
- Maximum height: 3.000 in
- Minimum underside clearance: 0.375 in
- Minimum inside wheel-to-wheel clearance: 1.750 in
- Maximum allowable mass/weight: 5.000 oz
- Official scale determines compliance with the 5 oz limit.
- A measurement box is used to check dimensional compliance.

PUBLISHED SPEED TIPS - ADVISORY, NOT RULES
The source separately recommends:
- Competitive cars generally weigh the maximum 5.0 oz.
- Concentrate weight as far rearward as possible without causing the car to pop a wheelie.
- Design a way to add/remove weight for final scale adjustment.
- Suggested ballast examples include washers, coins, embedded metal, fishing weights, pinewood derby weights, and embellishments.
- Reduce axle friction by removing burrs, polishing axles, and using a light lubricant such as graphite.
- Minor cleanup of wheel plastic stubs/flat spots/flashing is suggested while avoiding resizing/reshaping the wheel.
- Aerodynamic drag is described as very small, with streamlined shape and sleek finish suggested for reducing it.
- Check axle slots for square alignment and check wheels on a flat surface before gluing.

MODELING CONSEQUENCES
The facts above justify a data-driven CCCS/AWANA ruleset. They do NOT establish the detailed physical properties of the official wood, wheels, axles, starter gate, or track. Those remain separate characterization tasks.
