BOTTOM-LINE FINDING
GRAVITY RACER is not a clean or exclusive brand candidate. The exact phrase is already widely used for video games, software/interactive experiences, educational STEM material, toys, kits, CAD projects, and the generic name of a category of motorless racing vehicle. The compound GRAVITYRACER is also used commercially for toy products. Those uses create material common-law, marketplace-confusion, search-discoverability, app-store, domain, and registrability risks even if an exact live federal registration is not found.

DESCRIPTIVENESS / STRENGTH
GRAVITY RACER directly names a gravity-powered racer and is used as a common category term. For software that designs and simulates gravity racers, the wording strongly describes the subject and purpose. This can make the wording difficult to monopolize and difficult to rank distinctly in general and marketplace search.

TAGLINES
No exact web result was surfaced for REAL PHYSICS. REAL CREATIVITY. REAL RACERS. in the searches performed. That makes the phrase more promising than GRAVITY RACER at the preliminary level. It is not cleared until federal, state, common-law, advertising, app-marketplace, and international searches are completed for exact and similar wording.

DESIGN. SIMULATE. BUILD. RACE. describes the workflow and is likely weak as a source identifier. Treat it as explanatory copy, not the main protectable asset, unless counsel advises otherwise.

AWANA BRANDING
Awana states that Awana and the Awana logo are registered trademarks and limits use. Its guidelines allow some references and certain church/noncommercial uses, but the commercial/public software mockup should not display the Awana logo or imply endorsement without written permission. Use a neutral ruleset label during development, such as “CCCS local kit rules,” and obtain specific permission before shipping an AWANA-branded mode.

GRANDPRIX RACE MANAGER VISUAL CROSSOVER
Maintain only broad genre familiarity. Do not copy logos, exact screen arrangements, proprietary artwork, icon assets, or distinctive trade dress. Gravity Racer should remain independently branded and should not imply compatibility, sponsorship, or common origin.

LOGO ASSESSMENT
The checkered-flag motif and italicized motorsport lettering are common racing imagery. They provide low inherent distinctiveness and may resemble many racing brands. Create a unique device mark tied to the product’s special proposition: AI-assisted physical design, mass/CG visualization, virtual-to-real build, or a distinctive gravity/vector motif. Avoid AWANA wheel logos and third-party marks in product artwork.

RECOMMENDED DISPOSITION
Do not launch or file the product under GRAVITY RACER as the sole primary word mark without attorney-led comprehensive clearance. The current evidence shows heavy exact marketplace use in closely related games, software, toys, STEM education and gravity-racing contexts.

Best path:
1. Treat Gravity Racer as an internal working title.
2. Select a distinctive coined or suggestive primary mark.
3. Use “gravity-racer design and simulation” descriptively beneath the new mark.
4. Search the new candidate across USPTO federal records, state registrations, common-law web use, domains, social handles, app stores, game stores, company names and international databases.
5. Clear the final logo separately from the word mark.
6. Obtain explicit Awana permission before using its name/logo as branded product content.
7. File an intent-to-use application in the appropriate classes only after counsel approves the mark and goods/services description.
