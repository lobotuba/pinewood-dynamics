2. PRODUCT VISION AND PRINCIPLES
============================================================

2.1 Vision
A creativity-first 3D engineering game and design platform for gravity-powered derby cars.
A player imagines a car, shapes an editable design, configures real physical components,
simulates it against a modeled track and ruleset, analyzes and improves it while keeping its
look, exports it for real construction, and compares the real race with the prediction.

Core promise: Design what you imagine. Engineer it to race. Build it for real.
Brand tagline (section 4): Real Physics. Real Creativity. Real Racers.

2.2 Primary experience loop
IMAGINE -> DESIGN -> CONFIGURE -> RACE -> ANALYZE -> OPTIMIZE -> RACE AGAIN -> BUILD ->
MEASURE -> REAL-WORLD RACE -> COMPARE -> ITERATE

2.3 Core principles (frozen)
1. Creativity precedes optimization.
2. Physics determines performance; no purchasable or assigned performance statistics (VIS-002).
3. Rules constrain designs but do not define optimal designs.
4. Optimization improves the player's car instead of replacing the idea.
5. Every physical component has geometry, mass and position where material.
6. Simulation results must be explainable from solver telemetry; never invent causes.
7. Unknowns stay identified as unknown or estimated.
8. The digital design must remain buildable by the selected build capability.
9. As-designed and as-built states remain distinct.
10. Real-world testing is the final authority.
11. STL is an export, never the master design (GEO-001).
12. One shared constraint service governs generation, editing, optimization, inspection,
    simulation readiness and export (section 49 of the former baseline).
13. Published rules and local/frozen constraints remain visibly distinct.
14. A control exists only when the physics that gives it an effect exists (WHL-009).

============================================================

3.1 Platform (DES, owner decision 2026-10-27)
- PLT-002: The application shall be a TypeScript application rendering 3D with Three.js in
  a WebGL2-capable browser. A desktop wrapper (Tauri or Electron, TBD) may package the same
  build for desktop stores.
- PLT-003: The application shall run fully offline once loaded. No account, sign-in or
  network service is required for any MVP feature.
- PLT-004: The MVP shall collect no personal information and send no telemetry. Projects
  are stored locally (browser storage plus user-initiated file save/load). This keeps
  child-privacy obligations minimal until online features are deliberately added (PRV-001).
- PLT-005: Supported browsers, minimum GPU and performance targets: TBD, to be baselined at
  the Prototype gate from measured performance on owner-selected reference machines.

3.2 Software structure (PROPOSED)
Separate packages with one-way dependencies:
- core/units, core/provenance: quantity types carrying unit and provenance.
- kit: official-kit component library (block, wheel, axle) with provenance (KIT-001).
- geometry: master car representation and solid evaluation (section 6).
- mass: mass properties from geometry and components.
- rules: data-driven rulesets and the shared constraint service.
- track: track library and profiles.
- physics: the authoritative deterministic solver. No dependency on rendering.
- app: UI, Three.js renderer, race presentation.
The physics, mass and rules packages shall run headless under unit tests.

3.3 Key third-party components (PROPOSED)
- Three.js (MIT licence): rendering.
- manifold-3d (Apache-2.0 licence, WebAssembly): guaranteed-manifold solid Booleans, mass
  properties support and STL output.
- Licence notices of all bundled dependencies shall ship with the product.

3.4 Determinism (refines PHY-005)
Race results shall be reproducible bit-for-bit for the same build on the same JavaScript
engine. Across engines, results shall agree within a stated tolerance, to be measured and
frozen at the Physics Alpha gate. Transcendental math differences between engines are the
known source of cross-engine variation.

6. MASTER CAR MODEL (resolves GEO-004 / M-08)
============================================================

6.1 Representation (PROPOSED, GEO-009)
The authoritative body is a profile-intersection solid:

  Body = Block
         ∩ Extrude_Y(SideProfile)      (outline seen from the left, swept across the width)
         ∩ Extrude_Z(TopProfile)       (outline seen from above, swept through the height)
         [∩ Extrude_X(FrontProfile)]   (optional, later release)
         − SubtractiveFeatures         (bores, pockets, plane/wedge cuts, axle slots kept)

  Car = Body + Components              (wheels, axles, ballast, hardware, accessories)

Profiles are closed 2D curves (polyline plus arcs and smooth splines). Components are
separate rigid parts with their own geometry, material, mass and pose.

Rationale:
- Side and top cutting templates are the profiles themselves (BLD-002).
- A kid with a coping saw can build what the profiles describe: two saw cuts, then drilling.
- Mass properties and CG are computed from a guaranteed-manifold solid (GEO-002).
- AI generation (later release) reduces to drawing two outlines around fixed axle datums
  and protected zones, which is checkable (AI-002, LOC-AX-005, GATE-002).
- STL is a direct export of the evaluated solid (BLD-001).

Limits, stated openly: freeform sculpted shapes (such as the mockup's banana) are not
representable. Fillets/roundovers are limited to edge-profile operations in a later
release. Freeform sculpting stays a later-release candidate.

6.2 Immutable reference block (GEO-005)
The official kit block is stored separately from the derived body. Removed volume, operation
history, undo and build sequence derive from block + profiles + features.

6.3 Coordinate system and datums (adopted from C-03, PROPOSED for freeze, GEO-008/GEO-010)
- Origin: centre of the nominal rear axle-slot axis, centred laterally.
- +X toward the front; +Y toward the car's left; +Z up, normal to the nominal block bottom.
- Front axle datum: nominal front axle-slot axis. Rear axle datum: origin.
- Nominal wheelbase WB = X(front datum) − X(rear datum) (LOC-WB-001).
- Datum coordinates come from measurement of the official kit (CHAR-002); until then they
  are UNKNOWN and any placeholder is labelled DEFAULT (LOC-AX-004).

6.4 Units (resolves GEO-006 / M-10, PROPOSED)
- Internal master units: SI (metre, kilogram, second, radian).
- Display units: METRIC (mm, g) by default; ENGLISH (in, oz) selectable at any time as a
  user preference (owner decision 2026-10-27, D-04; GEO-011).
- Conversion constants are exact: 1 in = 0.0254 m; 1 oz (avoirdupois) = 0.028349523125 kg.
- Rule limits are stored in their source units and source precision, converted exactly.
  Limits are displayed in the selected unit system with the source value alongside when the
  two differ, e.g. "≥ 9.525 mm (3/8 in)". A converted limit that is not exact at display
  precision is marked "≈", e.g. "≤ ≈141.748 g (5.000 oz)". PASS/FAIL always comes from the
  unrounded comparison, and the section 9.7 rounding rules apply in both unit systems.
- STL export writes millimetres by default and states the unit in the file name and build
  sheet; the unit choice is explicit at export (section 35 former baseline).

6.5 Valid-solid acceptance (GEO-002, GEO-003)
Manifold, watertight, no self-intersection, consistent orientation, single connected body
unless components are explicitly separate, no wheel/body interference, exportable. Minimum
wall and feature sizes are TBD from manufacturing characterization (CHAR-007).

6.6 Tolerances (GEO-007)
Fields shall exist for datum lock, geometry comparison, tessellation, contact, mass
precision and print scale. Values are TBD until characterized; no invented tolerances.

7. COMPONENTS AND MASS
============================================================

7.1 Body mass (MAT-001, MAT-002)
m_body = rho_body * V_body. Nominal density is DEFAULT/ESTIMATED. Measured mode derives
effective density from the measured uncut block mass and block volume and is labelled
DERIVED. This is a uniform-density approximation and does not establish exact finished CG.

7.2 Component mass model
m_total = m_body + m_wheels + m_axles + m_ballast + m_hardware + m_accessories + m_finish
The player does not type total mass as a primary control. Paint, glue, decals and lubricant
contribute with provenance and uncertainty where represented (MAT-004).

7.3 Ballast (BAL-001..003)
Physical components with material, density, geometry, dimensions, mass, pose, mounting
method. Shapes: cylinder, disk, rectangular block, plate (MVP); sphere, washer, putty,
custom (later). Mounting: top, underside, side, internal cavity, hybrid. External mounting
is first class; the optimizer never assumes wood must be removed.

7.4 Materials (BAL-002, MAT-005, SAF-001)
MVP materials: tungsten, lead, steel, brass, zinc, tin, copper, aluminium, custom.
Lead is INCLUDED (owner decision 2026-10-27, D-06). Because the product is child-facing,
lead carries these content rules (SAF-003, implements SAF-001):
- A safety notice appears whenever lead is selected or placed, and in the build package:
  an adult handles lead, wash hands after handling, keep it away from mouths, do not sand.
- The product gives no instructions for melting, casting, cutting, drilling, filing or
  sanding lead. Lead is used only as whole pre-formed weights, inserted into a drilled
  cavity or held by mounting hardware.
- The build package marks every lead component "adult installs".
- A non-lead alternative of equal mass (e.g. tungsten, with its volume) is shown beside
  each lead component so the choice is informed.
- Lead is available at every experience level; at KID level it carries the adult-install
  tag.
- The policy text is reviewed before public release (18.5); jurisdiction-specific warning
  requirements are a counsel question.
Each material record carries density source, alloy/grade, nominal vs measured status,
safety class and generic/commercial status.

7.5 Mass margin (MAT-006, CMP-007)
The build target mass is set explicitly by the player and shown with its margin below the
limit. No hidden default margin. If the predicted-mass uncertainty band reaches the limit,
an ADVISORY is shown. A design at exactly the limit is never described as safe to build.

7.6 Centre of gravity and inertia
r_CG = sum(m_i r_i) / sum(m_i), shown as X from rear axle datum, Y, Z, updating live while
dragging components. Inertia tensor computed for the solver where used.

8. WHEELS AND AXLES
============================================================

8.1 Representation (WHL-001)
LF, RF, LR, RR assemblies independently represented: wheel mass, diameter, width, bore,
rotational inertia, runout; axle diameter, pose, straightness, friction properties.
Official-wheel width, diameter and mass are protected (RUL-008).

8.2 Wheel inertia (WHL-008)
Measured or calculated with documented method and uncertainty. Mass and radius alone are not
sufficient. Until CHAR-003 completes, inertia is ESTIMATED from geometry and labelled.

8.3 Axle friction (FRI-001)
MVP model: Coulomb friction at the axle-bore contact, torque = mu_a * N_i * r_axle per wheel,
where N_i is the modeled wheel load. mu_a is a per-car value with provenance (ESTIMATED until
calibrated by coast-down or track runs, CHAR-006), selectable by named preparation state
(e.g. "polished + graphite"). The UI shall not show a per-wheel friction slider; a single
calibrated value per preparation state is shown, with its provenance. Speed dependence and
hub/head rubbing are later refinements.

8.4 Alignment controls deferred (WHL-009, PROPOSED; modifies WHL-002 and the MVP list)
Camber, toe, rail-riding and three-wheel setups shall not be offered as controls until the
solver implements lateral dynamics and guide-rail contact (P6, P7). Until then a control
would have no physical effect or a fabricated one, which violates VIS-002. Wheel contact
state per wheel (WHL-004) is still modeled, and the inspector reports contact status.
Legality of camber, bent axles, drilled wheels, modified slots and three-wheel running for
the target event is UNKNOWN (WHL-005, WHL-004) and must be sourced from the event.

8.5 Inside wheel spacing (WHL-007)
Computed from actual installed wheel, axle and body geometry, not entered independently.

8.6 Sidewall marking (RUL-009, WHL-003)
The event rules require the kit maker's name to remain visible on the wheel sidewall. The
model stores "required sidewall marking: preserved" as a protected wheel attribute and the
inspector reports it in neutral words ("Wheel sidewall marking left intact"). Wheels are
always drawn with a plain sidewall; no third-party mark is rendered (BRD-007).

9.5 Constraint classes and precedence (section 49.1, CMP-003)
Classes: PUBLISHED RULE, LOCAL/FROZEN CONSTRAINT, DESIGN ADVISORY.

9.6 Protected zones (section 49.4)
Starting-pin contact zone; axle/slot structural zones; official wheel swept envelopes;
minimum inside-wheel envelope; underside/guide-rail clearance envelope; overall dimension
envelope.

9.7 Inspection measurement definitions (CMP-004)
Every published racing rule is enforced (owner decision 2026-10-27, D-11). Where the
event's measuring method is not published, the inspector uses the strictest reasonable
reading and says so on the inspection line (CMP-009). Only the event's written method
(CHAR-008) replaces that reading.
- Overall dimensions: axis-aligned in the car frame, including wheels, axles, ballast and
  every attached part, at the car's longest, widest and tallest points (the measurement
  box sees everything).
- Underside clearance: minimum height above the wheel contact plane of any point of the car
  other than the wheel treads, over the full length of the car rather than only between
  the axles, because the guide rail runs the whole length.
- Inside wheel spacing: minimum distance between inner faces of opposing wheels as installed.
- Pass/fail is evaluated on unrounded values at full precision (CMP-005).
- Limits are displayed exactly as the source states them: "≥ 0.375 in", never "≥ 0.38".
  (CMP-006)
- Measured values are displayed rounded toward failure: a minimum-type value rounds down, a
  maximum-type value rounds up. A failing value can never display as a passing number.
- A value equal to the limit passes ("at least", "maximum").
- When provenance or uncertainty means the true value may straddle the limit, the line is
  PROVISIONAL with an ADVISORY.

9.8 Expanded inspection (section 50)
Sections: KIT, GEOMETRY, CHASSIS/LOCAL LOCKS, START GATE, WHEELS, WEIGHT. Each line shows
its provenance class. Checks include axle datums unchanged, wheelbase unchanged, wheel
envelopes clear, gate contact valid, no pointed nose, no upward-sloping nose, legal
dimensions and clearances, official-wheel properties preserved, total mass ≤ limit.

9.9 Starting gate (RUL-007, GATE-001..004)
Published functional rule enforced as a protected gate-contact region at the front. Exact
pin geometry, contact height, nose radius and test method remain UNKNOWN (CHAR-004). Gate
check and axle/wheelbase locks are independent and fail independently.

============================================================
11. PHYSICS
============================================================

11.1 Authority (section 19)
Race outcomes come from a transparent, testable, deterministic solver. The renderer only
visualizes solver state. Game physics engines are not used for race outcomes.

11.2 MVP solver (PROPOSED, PHY-009; freezes PHY-004 for the MVP)
- The car is a rigid body whose front and rear axle contact points both stay on the lane
  profile. Pitch is therefore kinematic, set by the profile and the wheelbase.
- One generalized coordinate: rear-axle arc position s along the lane. Speed v = ds/dt.
- Wheels roll without slip: omega_i = v / r_i.
- Equation of motion from Newton-Euler with rolling constraints (equivalent to Lagrange), with
  gravity acting on the actual CG position (so CG height and rearward CG matter, which is
  the mechanism behind rear-weighting), translational and pitch kinetic energy, wheel
  rotational energy sum(1/2 I_i omega_i^2), and the loss mechanisms below. Acceleration and
  axle loads are solved together each instant. Derivation and verification:
  docs/physics-model.md.
- Loss mechanisms (each separable and switchable, PHY-003):
  axle-bore Coulomb friction (section 8.3) using wheel loads N_i;
  rolling/tread loss (coefficient ESTIMATED);
  aerodynamic drag F = 1/2 rho_air Cd A v^2 with Cd*A ESTIMATED by shape class and
  reported with sensitivity bounds (AERO-001).
- Wheel loads N_i come from the rigid-body balance on the actual track slope and curvature,
  including centripetal loading in the transition and the longitudinal acceleration term
  (PHY-007).
- Validity monitor: if a computed axle load goes negative (wheel lift) or the body would
  touch the track, the run is flagged OUTSIDE MODEL VALIDITY rather than continued as if
  valid.
- Lateral motion, yaw, roll, camber, toe and guide-rail contact are NOT modeled in the MVP
  and the UI says so in ENGINEERING level.

11.3 Numerics (PHY-005)
Integrator, step control and event localization are chosen at implementation and recorded;
convergence is demonstrated by step-halving; the energy ledger residual is reported for
every run. Thresholds are frozen at the Physics Alpha gate from measured convergence, not
guessed.

11.4 Energy ledger (ANA-001)
Potential -> translational + wheel rotational + axle loss + rolling loss + aero loss (+ rail
loss later) + numerical residual. Shown in results and engineering views.

11.5 Validation gates (section 22, GOV-006)
P0 track geometry; P1 ideal gravity and energy conservation; P2 wheel inertia; P3 axle
friction; P4 aerodynamics and limiting cases; P5 CG and rigid-body (pitch, loads);
P6 lateral dynamics; P7 guide-rail interaction; P8 manufacturing variation.
MVP requires P0-P5. Each stage needs analytic verification cases (e.g. frictionless ramp
time, rolling-cylinder inertia factor, constant-friction deceleration) before it is
treated as authoritative.

11.6 Real-world validation (PROPOSED, VAL-001)
Before comparing any prediction with a real run, the owner freezes the acceptance criterion
(e.g. predicted vs measured elapsed time error) in writing. The comparison is then run once
and recorded as pass or fail. Coefficients calibrated on one set of runs are validated on a
different set (calibration and validation data stay separate).

12.6 Build capability (resolves MFG-001 and the three tier lists, MFG-002; accepted in
principle by the owner, D-10)
Four tiers: BASIC (hand tools: coping saw, hand drill, sandpaper), INTERMEDIATE (adds
power drill, scroll/band saw), ADVANCED (drill press, precise layout, multi-setup work),

characterization. MVP: the side+top profile model is buildable at BASIC by construction;
pockets and bores flag the tier they require. "Easy/Medium" labels are retired.

12.7 Design modalities (MODE-005, DSN-001..006; owner decision 2026-10-27, D-05)
Two separate ways to design. Both write into the same project, and one car can use both.
A. SHAPE BY REMOVAL. Start from the kit block and take wood away: side and top outlines,
   bores, pockets, plane cuts (6.1, 12.3). At KID level this is presented as picking a cut
   shape or drawing a saw line and drilling holes, on the same underlying model.
B. BUILD BY ADDING PARTS. The KID-level modality, also available at BUILDER and
   ENGINEERING. Snap pieces onto the body from a parts library, for example building-brick
   style blocks, windows and canopies, trees, figures, fins, wings, lights. Library content
   is open and grows over time (D-12).
Requirements for added parts:
- DSN-001: Every part is a physical component with geometry, material, mass with
  provenance, attachment method (glue, pin, screw, double-sided tape) and a real-world
  source: a craft item to buy, a 3D-printable STL, or a hand-made piece with a template.
- DSN-002: Parts attach to snap points on the body surface or on other parts. In CERTIFIED
  context they cannot be placed into protected zones (wheel envelopes, gate-contact zone,
  underside clearance, overall envelope).
- DSN-003: Parts count fully toward mass, CG, overall dimensions, clearance and the
  aerodynamic estimate. They have no performance values of their own (VIS-002): a tree on
  the roof costs time only through the mass, CG and drag the solver computes.
- DSN-004: Part names and shapes are generic. No third-party brands or trade dress (for
  example no LEGO name or stud logo, no licensed characters).
- DSN-005: The build package lists every added part with how to get or make it; printable
  parts export as STL; hand-made parts get a 1:1 template.
- DSN-006: A part that cannot be made with the selected build capability is flagged.

19. PHYSICAL CHARACTERIZATION PLAN (CHAR-001..008)
============================================================

These measurements resolve most open technical items. Record each value with unit, method,
instrument and date; provenance MEASURED.

CHAR-001 Block: length, width, height, slot positions; mass of 3-5 uncut blocks (scale to
  0.01 g or better).
CHAR-002 Axle datums: slot centre positions relative to block rear and bottom; derive WB.
CHAR-003 Wheels and axles: mass, outer diameter, tread width, bore, hub geometry for 3-5 of
  each; wheel inertia by bifilar/trifilar pendulum or spin-down; axle diameter and mass.
CHAR-004 Start gate: pin diameter, height above track, position, release motion (photos
  with scale, slow-motion video).
CHAR-005 Track: elevation of the lane surface every ~1 ft (closer through the transition)
  from start pin to finish; rail height and width; lane width; timer sensor position.
CHAR-006 Friction: coast-down or track runs at two or more car masses with known
  preparation state; phone slow-motion video for position vs time.
CHAR-007 Tolerances: repeatability of the owner's measurements and of a typical home build.
CHAR-008 Event: written rules from the actual target event; confirm track model and length;
  ask whether camber, bent axles, three-wheel running and slot changes are allowed.
