GRAVITY RACER - RTM FINDINGS INCORPORATION CHANGELOG

The Missing and Conflicting Requirements Assessment has been incorporated into GravityRacer_Requirements_Traceability_Matrix.txt.

ADDED REQUIREMENT FAMILIES
- CMP: compliance/rules governance
- KIT: official-kit component identity
- MFG: manufacturability
- SAF: child-facing material safety
- FRI: friction-model definition
- GATE/TIM/LANE: start, finish and lane modeling
- AERO: aerodynamic fidelity
- DAT: project data and migration
- PLT: platform/performance
- ACC: accessibility
- PRV: privacy/community gating
- GOV: baseline, bidirectional traceability and release governance
- CHAR: physical-characterization dependencies

KEY CONFLICT RESOLUTIONS CAPTURED
- Pointed/upturned starting-nose conflict is tied to RUL-010 and compliance qualification.
- Binary legal status is replaced by multi-state compliance requirements.
- Fixed wheelbase now depends explicitly on a frozen coordinate/datum convention.
- Fore/aft axle movement is incompatible with certified fixed-wheelbase mode.
- Inside wheel spacing must be computed from actual installed geometry.

STATUS
New findings are incorporated as DRAFT requirements. Unknown numerical tolerances, track/gate geometry, kit dimensions and unresolved event legality remain TBD and are explicitly traced through CHAR requirements.
