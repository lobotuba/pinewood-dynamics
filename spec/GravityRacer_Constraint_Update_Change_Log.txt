KEY DECISIONS
- The published rule that the front should not come to a point or slope upward so that the starting peg can retain the car is treated as a PUBLISHED RULE.
- Exact starting-pin/contact dimensions remain UNKNOWN until measured or authoritatively specified.
- Fixed official-kit axle locations and derived nominal wheelbase are treated as LOCAL/FROZEN CONSTRAINTS, because the current published rules page does not explicitly state the unchanged-wheelbase requirement.
- One shared constraint service shall govern AI generation, manual editing, optimization, technical inspection, simulation readiness, and manufacturing export.
- Orbit View shall expose axle datums, wheelbase dimension, starting-contact zone, wheel swept envelopes, axle structural zones, and guide-rail clearance.
- Certified Build export requires all published hard rules and active local/frozen constraints to pass.
