GRAVITY RACER - EXTERNAL KNOWNS
BESTTRACK CONFIGURATION BASELINE

SOURCE
BestTrack Pinewood Derby Track Specs
https://www.besttrack.com/track_specs.html

PUBLISHED TIMED DISTANCES
Measured from the start-gate pins to the middle timing hole:
- 35-ft configuration: 358 in (29 ft 10 in; derived SI conversion 9.0932 m)
- 42-ft configuration: 442 in (36 ft 10 in; derived SI conversion 11.2268 m)
- 49-ft configuration: 526 in (43 ft 10 in; derived SI conversion 13.3604 m)

PUBLISHED POST-CURVE STRAIGHT-SECTION CONFIGURATIONS
- 35-ft configuration: three 7-ft straight sections plus stop section.
- 42-ft configuration: four 7-ft straight sections plus stop section.
- 49-ft configuration: five 7-ft straight sections plus stop section.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION
The named track configuration length is not the timed racing distance. The default finish trigger should use the published start-pin-to-middle-timing-hole distance unless a calibrated local track overrides it.

NOT ESTABLISHED BY THE CAPTURED SOURCE
- full vertical profile;
- starting height;
- transition radius/curve;
- guide-rail dimensions;
- lane width;
- start-pin geometry or motion;
- track surface/friction;
- lane-specific differences.

These remain UNKNOWN/UNCHARACTERIZED until authoritatively specified or measured.
